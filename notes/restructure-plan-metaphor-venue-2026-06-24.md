## Proposed structure (metaphor-studies version)
1. **Introduction** — open on the *theoretical* problem, not the dog. The gap: CMT and
   its current extensions describe metaphorical mappings and their contextual use, but
   don't explain what *maintains* a source domain's structure or why some mappings stay
   projectible across a community while others erode. State the contribution in one
   move (HPC supplies stability mechanisms + projectibility; recasts CMT). One sentence
   foreshadows the dog as a later cross-species test; no dog-led opening.
2. **The state of conceptual metaphor theory and what it still lacks** — merge current
   §2's "source domains without mechanisms" with a fair survey of where CMT is now.
   Establish the gap *against the current field*, not against 1980-vintage CMT.
3. **Situating the account** (NEW, the section the AE demanded) — the positioning
   blocks from the scaffold:
   - 3.1 Extended CMT (Kövecses 2020): online contextual selection across four
     schematicity levels vs HPC's diachronic stability. Redescription rebuttal:
     erosion order, conditioned conventionalization, cross-species recruitment.
   - 3.2 Deliberate metaphor theory (Steen 2015/2017): orthogonal communicative axis;
     the 2x2 (deliberate x mechanism-support). Cite Flusberg et al. 2018 + the
     Gibbs/Thibodeau caution.
   - 3.3 Cultural and cross-linguistic variation (Kövecses 2005; discourse work):
     HPC's built-in variation story (same bodies, different mechanisms). Pull the
     "different communities metaphorize differently" material up from current §2.
   - 3.4 Concept structure and analogy: Murphy & Medin 1985 theory-theory as the
     ancestor; HPC as its world-side completion; relation to Gentner/Holyoak (already
     engaged in current §5). This absorbs the AE's concept-learning demand.
4. **HPC and theory-constitutive metaphor** (Boyd) — current §3, largely intact.
5. **Metaphor as cross-domain HPC extension** — current §4, intact (the reframing).
6. **Consequences and predictions** — current §5; tie the dispute-recasting back to §3
   so positioning and payoff connect.
7. **The full range: literary, everyday, constitutive** — current §6; add the Steen
   deliberateness cross-tabulation here (links to 3.2).
8. **A cross-species test case: canine trail cognition** — current §7+§8, demoted and
   trimmed (target ~30-40% shorter). Frame explicitly as *one* illustration that the
   operation is not language-specific, not as the paper's reason for being. Keep the
   painted-line prediction, the HPC-structure evidence, POWER IS UP; cut or compress
   the heavy stats recitation (Thesen/Hepper/Cafazzo/Fagan) to what the argument needs.
9. **Conclusion** — current §9; reorder so the metaphor-theory contribution leads and
   the dog is the closing illustration, not the frame.
